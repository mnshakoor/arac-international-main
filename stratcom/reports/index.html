UNGA 81 Field Report: Diaspora Protest Mobilization in New York City

By M. Nuri Shakoor, SRMP-R · MNS Consulting · Published September 27, 2026 · 12 min read

A field-evidence account of diaspora protest activity observed during UN General Assembly 81, drawn from contemporaneous photographs, video, and handwritten field notes covering Guinea, Liberia, Haiti, Bangladesh, North Korea, and a Philippines-led coalition. The underlying case study for ARAC’s OODA-Risk framework.
